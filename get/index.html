Play Hub

A little app store for your Android phone, tablet or Windows computer. Get Gambit chess and more, and keep them up to date with one tap.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If your phone asks, tap Download again.
  2. When it's done, tap Open (or open hub.apk from your Downloads).
  3. If Android says your browser can't install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details and then Install anyway. Play Hub isn't from the Play Store, so this is normal.
  5. Open Play Hub, pick an app, and tap Install. Play Hub keeps your apps updated.

Install on Windows

  1. Open this page in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome and click Get it for Windows.
  2. Play Hub opens. Click the gold Install button at the top, then Install again in the little box.
  3. That's it. Play Hub now has its own icon in the Start menu and opens in its own window. No warnings, and it updates by itself.
  4. Click Open on Gambit to play. Some apps, like Quillsong, are Android only.
Play Hub doesn't work on iPhone or iPad yet.
